## Upstream and shader tools

[UPSTREAM_PROVENANCE.json](UPSTREAM_PROVENANCE.json) records the SuperMarioWonderRecomp upstream repository, its commit and MIT license, plus the Vulkan-Headers version used by a private build dependency. That is provenance, not a blanket license for unrelated material. Review the shader tool notices: [Ryujinx](../tools/bayonetta3_shaders/LICENSE.Ryujinx), [SMW](../tools/bayonetta3_shaders/LICENSE.SMW), [bayonetta3-mobile](../tools/bayonetta3_shaders/LICENSE.bayonetta3-mobile) and [d2r-mobile](../tools/bayonetta3_shaders/LICENSE.d2r-mobile). The included [`kraken_worker_ooz.cpp`](../tools/bayonetta3_shaders/kraken_worker_ooz.cpp) adapts the MIT-licensed bayonetta3-mobile worker, and its notice is retained above. The repository does not supply external Ryujinx sources or DLLs, the Kraken decoder source, or a compiled worker. Obtain those under their own terms and check compatibility before distributing a combined artifact. One caution: ooz's `kraken.cpp` states GPL-3.0-or-later, while the terms of other files in the revision that was tested still need independent verification.

The FSR shader sources and their embedded generated form reference AMD FidelityFX FSR 1.0; keep the [AMD notice](../tools/shaders/fsr/LICENSE.AMD). The BC7 partition tables in [`nvn_bc.cpp`](../runtime/src/nvn/nvn_bc.cpp) are described there as derived using Pillow's reference decoder. The [Pillow license](../runtime/src/nvn/LICENSE.Pillow) is included for attribution, but the exact Pillow version and the full derivation are still unverified. [`vk_gen.cpp`](../runtime/src/vk_gen.cpp) is generated from the Khronos Vulkan registry, and the [Vulkan-Headers notice](../runtime/src/LICENSE.Vulkan-Headers) preserves the MIT option and registry notice for the recorded v1.4.357 dependency. Whether that registry version is the one actually used to generate the checked-in wrapper has not been confirmed.

The original source inputs for the presentation shader were not recovered. The current [`present.vert`](../runtime/shaders/present.vert) and [`present.frag`](../runtime/shaders/present.frag) were written after examining the previously embedded SPIR-V and the runtime interface, and [`shaders_spv.h`](../runtime/src/shaders_spv.h) was regenerated from those reconstructed sources. They are not recovered originals and not a clean-room implementation. Static comparison and compiler details are in [BUILD.md](BUILD.md#presentation-shader-regeneration); that comparison is neither a GPU rendering test nor a legal determination. Before distributing the source or generated header, review the provenance and rights of the original material, including whether the reconstructed sources satisfy any corresponding-source obligations, and keep this provenance next to the applicable notices.
